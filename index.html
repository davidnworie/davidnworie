Geoscientist at ExxonMobil  ·  Sedimentology  ·  Subsurface Characterization

David Chibuzor Nworie, Ph.D.

I'm a geoscientist with expertise in subsurface characterization, sedimentology and uncertainty evaluation. My research looks at how sediment moves through deepwater systems, from grain-scale flow physics to basin-scale reservoir architecture, and I try to put that understanding to work on practical subsurface problems.
