Leadership & impact

Beyond the lab

Green Network Initiative · Convener

I convene the Green Network Initiative (GNI), a hub for young leaders passionate about driving societal change and connecting scientific knowledge with community impact.

Why it mattersIt gives young people a place to connect, learn from each other and work on projects in their communities

AGU Congressional Visits Day · Group lead, 2024

I served as group lead for the Colorado delegation during the American Geophysical Union's Congressional Visits Day, meeting congressional offices on Capitol Hill.

Why it mattersIt was a chance to explain to policymakers, first-hand, why Earth science research matters to their constituents

Graduate Student Government · Director of Student Affairs, 2024–25

Served as Director of Student Affairs in the Graduate Student Government at the Colorado School of Mines.

Global Student Ambassador · 2023–24

Served as a Global Student Ambassador for the Colorado School of Mines.

Professional societies & outreach

Giving back to the geoscience community

Across Nigeria, Brazil and the United States, I've helped run the student and professional societies that connect students with industry: organizing events, managing finances and leading chapters. Much of my own early career came through these networks, and I try to give some of that back.

NABG · Session chair, 2024

Chaired a session at the National Association of Black Geoscientists' 43rd Technical Conference featuring the Director of the USGS and AGU's Assistant Director for Grants and Education Programs.

NABG · Field trip facilitator, 2023

Facilitated the field trip at NABG's 42nd Technical Conference.

SPE Ambassador Lecturer & Energy4me Ambassador

Volunteer lecturer with the Society of Petroleum Engineers' Ambassador Lecturer Program (since 2019) and Energy4me Ambassador (since 2021), educating the public about energy. Public Relations Manager, SPE UNICAMP Student Chapter.

NAPE

Provost of NAPE Young Professionals (2020); Event Planning Community Lead (2019–20); President (2018–19) and Chief Financial Officer (2016–18) of the Ebonyi State University student chapter; NAPE/AAPG field trip volunteer (2019).

AAPG & EAGE

Student Chapter President, AAPG Ebonyi State University (2017–19); member, AAPG Africa Region YP/Students Sub-committee (2018–19); AAPG YP Brasil volunteer (2021–); Chapter President, EAGE.

Community

President (2019–20) and Ambassador of the Pan African Institute for Global Affairs and Strategy; Rotaract community development volunteer (2018–22).

Undergraduate research judge

Judge at the Colorado School of Mines Undergraduate Research Symposium, 2023 and 2025.

i-Scholar Initiative

Member of the Scholarship Review Board (2022–present) and Scholarship Management Committee (2022–23).
