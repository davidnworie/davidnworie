Journal article

Carbonate turbidity currents play an underappreciated role in the global carbon cycle

Nworie, C. D., Jobe, Z. R., Slootman, A., Venturelli, R., Eggenhuisen, J. T., & Reijmer, J. J. G. · The Sedimentary Record, 24(1)

Flume experiments with natural carbonate sediments at sediment concentrations from 1.1% to 11% show concentration-dependent flow structure, sediment transport and carbon partitioning, highlighting carbonate turbidity currents as an overlooked pathway for deep-sea carbon burial.

Why it mattersProvides experimental data on a carbon-transport pathway that has received little attention

doi:10.2110/001c.159298
