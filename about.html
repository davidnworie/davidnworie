My story

I'm a geoscientist at ExxonMobil in Spring, Texas, working with geological and geophysical datasets and integrating stratigraphy, sedimentology and quantitative analysis to support technical interpretation, risk assessment and decision-making.

I grew up in Lagos, Nigeria. At Ebonyi State University I studied Geology and Exploration Geophysics, led student chapters of NAPE, AAPG and EAGE, and gained early field experience in geological mapping, groundwater exploration and lead–zinc and barite exploration across southeastern and north-central Nigeria. My undergraduate thesis evaluated the geology and petrophysics of the Afikpo Sandstone. After graduating, I worked as a geoscientist at Degeconek in Lagos, characterizing hydrocarbon reservoirs from seismic, well and production data.

A FAPESP scholarship took me to Brazil, where I earned an M.Sc. in Petroleum Science and Engineering at the Universidade Estadual de Campinas (UNICAMP). As a graduate research fellow, I interpreted and modeled paleokarst features in pre-salt carbonate reservoirs of the Santos Basin using seismic and well data, built 3D geological models with geostatistical methods, and evaluated how paleokarst affects the distribution of reservoir flow properties.

I then completed my Ph.D. in Geology at the Colorado School of Mines (2026) as a first-generation doctoral scholar, first as a graduate teaching assistant and then as a graduate research assistant. My dissertation used laboratory flume experiments to understand how particle characteristics control sediment gravity flows, their deposits and reservoir quality. During my doctorate I was a visiting scholar at Utrecht University and completed geoscience internships at Shell, Chevron and BP.

Beyond research, I care about bridging science with community impact. I convene the Green Network Initiative, a hub for young leaders driving societal change, and I've served in student government, science-policy advocacy and professional societies. Outside work, I love traveling, meeting new people and building connections. I speak English and Yoruba professionally, with working Igbo and elementary Portuguese.
